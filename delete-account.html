MoodMeal Account Deletion

This page explains how MoodMeal users can request deletion of their account and associated data.

How to request deletion

  1. Use the email address registered with your MoodMeal account.
  2. Click the button below to email the MoodMeal support contact.
  3. Include your registered account email in the message. Do not send your password.
  4. We may contact you to verify that you control the account before processing the request.

Request account deletion by email

What will be deleted

After verifying the request, the MoodMeal team will manually review and process deletion of the user's Firebase Authentication account and associated user data that MoodMeal controls, where applicable.

Data that may be retained

Certain information may need to be retained for a limited period where required for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legal compliance. If any data is retained, it will be limited to the applicable purpose and retention requirement.

Processing and contact

Requests are reviewed manually. The team will aim to process verified requests promptly and contact the requester if additional information is needed. Do not include passwords or other sensitive authentication credentials in your request.
