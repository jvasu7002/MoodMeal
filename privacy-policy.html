MoodMeal Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Introduction

MoodMeal helps users discover food recommendations and nearby restaurants. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the app.

Information We Handle

  • Account information: information such as your email address and user identifier used to register and sign in, including through Firebase Authentication.
  • Location information: your device location when you grant location permission and use location-based features.
  • Food queries and preferences: the questions, food preferences, or other information you submit to request recommendations.
  • Technical information: technical or diagnostic information, if collected by the app or its service providers, to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

How Information Is Used

Information may be used to authenticate users, provide food recommendations, discover nearby restaurants, respond to requests, operate app features, maintain security, and troubleshoot problems.

Backend Processing

MoodMeal sends certain information to its backend server to provide app functionality. Depending on the feature you use, this may include your location information and food-related queries. The backend processes this information to support recommendations and nearby restaurant discovery.

Location Information

If you grant location permission, MoodMeal may access your device location and send it to its backend server to find nearby restaurants and provide location-based recommendations. You can manage location permission through your device settings. If you deny permission, some location-based features may not work as intended.

Third-Party Services and Sharing

MoodMeal relies on third-party services to support functions such as authentication, backend hosting, restaurant discovery, and AI-powered recommendations. Information may be transmitted to or processed by these services when needed to provide the requested features. Those providers' handling of information is subject to their own terms and privacy policies.

MoodMeal does not describe any information as anonymous or claim that it is never shared unless that has been verified for the app's actual implementation.

Data Retention

Information should be retained only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, to operate the service, resolve issues, meet legal obligations, or enforce applicable terms. Actual retention depends on the information and the services involved. Contact us for questions about a specific data-retention practice.

Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Choices and Requests

You can manage device permissions, including location permission, in your device settings. For questions or requests concerning your information, contact us using the details below.

Children's Privacy

MoodMeal should be used in accordance with applicable age requirements. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us so the concern can be reviewed.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when MoodMeal's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this page, with the updated date shown above.
